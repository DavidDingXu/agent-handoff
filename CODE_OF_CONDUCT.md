# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We want participation in agent-handoff to be respectful, technically honest, and safe for people sharing sensitive development context.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, constructive feedback;
- respecting different experience levels and communication styles;
- disclosing uncertainty and correcting mistakes openly;
- protecting private conversation data, credentials, and identifying details;
- focusing disagreement on evidence and behavior rather than people.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sustained disruption, publishing another person's private information, or knowingly posting real session data, credentials, or full active share links without permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions that violate this standard and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when needed to protect the community.

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the contact method on their GitHub profile. Security vulnerabilities must use the repository's private vulnerability reporting flow described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with as much confidentiality as practical.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, review discussions, project-managed community spaces, and public interactions where someone is representing this project.

This policy is adapted from the principles of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/), version 2.1.
